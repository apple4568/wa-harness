# Vegas CRM — access outreach log

Goal: obtain API read/write access to Vegas CRM, which sits inside Midam's 90-day
condition. See `deal_risks.md` R3 for why this is worth doing for the account but is
not company strategy.

## 2026-09-16 · Phone call, Bundang HQ (main office; second office in Busan)

**Asked for:** 제휴 담당자 / 사업개발.
**Outcome:** deflected. Told to send an email. Asked directly whether a manager could
be reached; answer was no, the information would be passed along internally.
**No name, title or direct address obtained.**

**Read:** this is the standard gatekeeper response at a Korean B2B company, not a
rejection. Nobody routes a cold caller to a partnerships manager. Not a signal about
the merits.

**Decision recorded:** did not walk into the Busan office. Busan is the wrong office
(partnership decisions sit at Bundang), an unannounced visit after a same-day
deflection reads as pushy rather than determined, and physical presence does not create
the leverage we lack — which is revenue attached to our name.

### Standing rule from this call

**Do not name Midam Clinic to Vegas.** Vegas already has an AI partner (name captured
as "Wise AI" / "WiseUp AI", unverified, reportedly focused on domestic inbound calls).
Telling Vegas that a named clinic wants Instagram DM automation with CRM write-back
hands a qualified opportunity to a vendor positioned to route it to their existing
partner. It also discloses a prospect's plans to their own vendor without consent.

Our approach stays generic. The clinic's approach carries the clinic's name — which is
theirs to give.

## The actual lever: a request from Midam, as Vegas's customer

To Vegas we are a stranger with no revenue attached; ignoring us is free. Midam is a
paying customer, and a customer asking for an integration is a retention issue.

Hand the 원장 ready-made text so it costs them 30 seconds. Never ask a client to compose
something.

> 안녕하세요. [병원명]입니다.
>
> 저희가 해외 환자(대만) 문의 응대 업무를 개선하기 위해 외부 솔루션을 도입 검토 중입니다.
> 해당 솔루션에서 베가스 CRM의 예약 조회 및 등록 연동이 필요한 상황입니다.
>
> 고객사 요청으로 API 연동이 가능한지, 가능하다면 절차가 어떻게 되는지 안내 부탁드립니다.
> 필요하시면 담당자 간 미팅도 가능합니다.
>
> 감사합니다.

Deliberately does not name us — it is their business need, which is both true and
stronger, and it keeps our name out of the competitor's line of sight until there is
something to see.
