# gtm/ — selling WA Harness / Synaptix into Korean aesthetic clinics

Working notes on reaching decision makers at Seoul (강남/서초/압구정) dermatology
clinics, replacing unstructured cold walk-ins. Product context lives in
`brand/spec/brand_spec.md` and `brand/research/`.

- `clinic_sales_playbook.md` — the diagnosis (a software vendor being rejected as a
  broker), repositioning away from a WhatsApp-shaped pitch, who actually buys
  (실장 → 원장), the mystery-shop wedge, and the three channels that reach a
  decision maker: 학회 booths, EMR integration as distribution, government B2B.
- `legal_pricing_constraints.md` — why "외국인환자 유치" is the worst possible opening
  line, and the 강남언니(유죄) vs 로톡(무죄) precedent that forces a flat-fee pricing
  model. Also 알림톡 정보성/광고성 and 의료법 56/57 limits on automated messages.
- `mystery_shop_audit.md` — spec for the inbound response-time audit: sample, probes,
  fields, ethics, and the two outputs (per-clinic sheet + aggregate report).
- `scripts_ko.md` — Korean scripts: walk-in, KakaoTalk follow-up, cold email,
  one-page handout structure, 학회 booth approach, partner/reseller ask.

Market snapshot (2026-09): 2.01M foreign patients in 2025, 87.2% treated in Seoul,
dermatology +86.2% YoY, China now the #1 source market ahead of Japan, and the 10%
VAT refund on foreign cosmetic procedures sunset at the end of 2025.
