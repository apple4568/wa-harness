# Getting in the door: South Korean skin clinics, foreign-patient intake

Working notes — how to convert cold walk-ins into 원장-level meetings.
All figures sourced; see `## Sources` at the bottom.

---

## 1. Why the current walk-in dies at the desk

The deflection is not indifference. It is a trained compliance reflex, and you are
triggering it with your own opening line.

Under the **의료 해외진출 및 외국인환자 유치 지원에 관한 법률** (의료해외진출법):

- Attracting foreign patients **without being a registered 유치업자 is a crime** —
  up to 3 years imprisonment or a ₩30M fine. Since the 2024 amendment, offenders'
  names are publicly disclosed and commissions earned are subject to confiscation.
- The **clinic is punished too** for accepting patients from an unregistered broker:
  corrective order or suspension of its foreign-patient business (1–3 months first
  offence, 3–6 months second), and registration cancellation for serious violations.

So when an unknown person walks in and says the words "외국인환자 유치", the desk
staff hear *broker with no registration number*. Saying "we don't do that" is the
cheapest correct answer available to them. They are not evaluating your offer —
they are closing a liability.

Three compounding problems:

| Problem | What they experience |
|---|---|
| **No credential** | Anonymous walk-in. Nothing to verify, nothing to file. |
| **No demand proof** | You are asking for their patients' revenue while bringing nothing. |
| **Wrong door** | 데스크 is not a channel to the 원장. It is the wall built to stop this exact conversation. |

## 2. The market you are walking into (use these numbers as your hook)

- **2025: 2.01 million foreign patients**, an all-time record (2024: 1.17M; 2023: 0.61M).
- **87.2% were treated in Seoul** (~1.755M) — 2,555 registered institutions, 62.5% of the national total.
- **Dermatology is the whole game**: 56.6% of all foreign patients in 2024 (705k),
  and the fastest-growing specialty in 2025 at **+86.2% YoY**. Derm + plastic ≈ 74%.
- **China overtook Japan for the first time in 2025** — the two together are 60.6% (~1.22M).
- Competitive reality: roughly **3,500 registered 유치의료기관 and 2,100 registered
  유치사업자** (2024). Registration gets you in the room; it does not differentiate you.

**The timing hook that actually earns a meeting:** the 10% VAT refund for foreign
cosmetic/derm procedures **sunset at the end of 2025** and was left out of the tax
bill. 대한성형외과의사회 estimates foreign patient volume dropped **20–30%** after it
ended. An extension bill (조세특례제한법 개정안, extending to 2027-12-31) is sitting in
subcommittee. Clinics just got 10% more expensive to foreigners overnight and are
feeling it right now. That is your conversation opener — not "do you do foreign patients?"

## 3. Fix #1 — Become legally payable before you pitch anything

This is the single highest-leverage move and everything else is downstream of it.
Register as a **외국인환자 유치업자** with the 시·도지사 via the KORP system
(medicalkorea.or.kr/korp).

Requirements:

- **자본금 ₩100M** (₩50M if you also hold 종합여행업 registration under 관광진흥법)
- **보증보험** covering damages to foreign patients, from an FSC-licensed insurer
- A physical **사무소**
- Registration renewal and annual performance reporting via KORP

Legal commission ceilings once registered (외국인환자 적정 유치 수수료율 고시):

| Institution type | Max commission as % of total treatment fee |
|---|---|
| 의원 (clinic — your targets) | **30%** |
| 병원 / 종합병원 | 20% |
| 상급종합병원 | 15% |

Exceeding the cap = registration cancellation + 과징금, and there is a whistleblower
reward program. Know this cold; quoting the cap correctly in the first 60 seconds
signals you are not a 브로커.

**What changes:** your opening line stops being a question and becomes a credential.
"등록 유치업자 제○○○○호입니다" is a sentence the desk can write down and pass upward.
A question is a sentence they can end.

## 4. Fix #2 — Stop using the front door

You said it yourself: they move on networks, not inbound. So buy your way into the
network. Three doors, ranked by how fast they open.

### Door A — Exhibit where the 원장들 physically walk past you

You do not chase 원장 one clinic at a time. You stand still at a conference and let
several hundred walk to you.

- **대한피부과의사회 추계학술대회** — 2026-12-11~13, COEX 마곡 컨벤션센터.
  **협력업체 (vendor booth) applications: 2026-06-01 ~ 10-30.** That window is open
  right now and closes in about six weeks. Attendee pre-registration 10-12 ~ 11-20.
- **대한피부과의사회 제28회 춘계학술대회** — 2026-03-29, 스위스그랜드호텔 (next cycle).
- **제24차 대경피부치료 학술대회** — 06-21, Daegu (regional, cheaper, less crowded).

A booth at the derm doctors' association conference is the only setting where a
cold approach to a 원장 is socially normal rather than suspicious. Budget for it;
it replaces roughly six months of walk-ins.

### Door B — Government-run B2B matchmaking (free or subsidised credibility)

These exist precisely because the government wants registered 유치업자 matched to
clinics. Being in the room is itself a vetting signal.

- **Medical Korea** (MOHW + KHIDI), COEX Seoul, every March — the 2026 edition ran
  19–22 March with 39 overseas buyer companies from 18 countries, 171 domestic
  sellers and **667 business-matching consultations**. Organising office:
  mkconf2026@gmail.com / 02-6215-0261.
- **서울의료관광 국제트래블마트 (SITMMT)** — 서울시 + 서울관광재단, held 09-08~09 2026
  at Ambassador Seoul Pullman: 70 overseas buyers, ~300 domestic sellers, B2B
  consultations + 홍보부스 + 네트워킹의 밤. Seller applications open late August.
  Just missed this year's — calendar it for August 2027 (sitmmt.org).
- **부산국제의료관광컨벤션 (BIMTC)** — August, Busan.
- **KHIDI 외국인환자 유치사업자 컨설팅 지원사업** and the 한국관광공사 의료관광
  유치업체 육성 프로그램 — subsidised consulting and product development for
  registered facilitators. 관광공사 의료관광비즈니스센터: 02-752-2102~4.

### Door C — Rent someone else's relationship with the 원장

Korean clinic B2B runs on introduction. Find the people who already have lunch with
the 대표원장 and trade with them:

- **Aesthetic laser / device sales reps and distributors** (클래시스, 원텍, 제이시스메디칼
  and their dealers). A rep covering Gangnam knows fifty 원장 by first name and visits
  them monthly. Their interest is aligned with yours: more foreign volume = more
  device consumables and more machines.
- **개원 컨설팅 / 인테리어 / 의료 전문 세무·노무사.** Industry write-ups on selling into
  clinics are blunt about this: long-term partnerships with 개원 컨설팅 firms, interior
  contractors and medical law offices make leads arrive automatically, and consultants
  who pair consulting with sales close ~27% better than cold reps.
- **병원 마케팅 대행사** already running the clinic's domestic ads. You are not competing
  with them — you are the international channel they cannot service.

Offer these partners a clean referral arrangement. Note the legal line: commission
for *foreign* patient introduction is lawful only between a registered 유치업자 and a
registered 유치의료기관; domestic patient referral fees remain prohibited under 의료법
제27조 제3항. Keep the two strictly separate.

## 5. Fix #3 — Reverse the ask: arrive with demand, not with a service

Every one of the ~2,100 registered facilitators walks in offering "we bring foreign
patients." That sentence is worth nothing because it is a promise.

Bring a booking instead.

> "3월 둘째 주에 오사카에서 오는 확정 환자 5명이 있습니다. 리쥬란·울쎄라 위주고 1인당
> 예상 객단가 150만 원선입니다. 일정 잡을 수 있는 원장님과 5분만 이야기하고 싶습니다."

That is not a favour request; it is inbound revenue with a date attached. Five real
patients is enough — you do not need scale, you need proof. Get those five from the
demand side first (a Japanese or Chinese community, a travel operator, a KOL, a
university exchange group, an expat network), even at zero margin.

**Pilot offer that removes all risk for them:** first 5 patients at **0% commission**,
you cover interpretation and accompaniment; if show-up and satisfaction hold, you sign
a standard contract at a normal rate. A 원장 can say yes to that without a meeting
with their accountant.

What clinics actually worry about, and what your one-pager must pre-answer:
no-shows and deposit handling, who interprets during consultation, who handles
post-procedure complications after the patient flies home, who is liable, and whether
the commission structure will survive an audit.

## 6. Fix #4 — Target only clinics that can legally say yes

Stop walking into random clinics. Build a list first.

1. **KORP registry (medicalkorea.or.kr/korp)** — search with blank fields to return
   *all* registered 유치의료기관. A clinic not on this list **cannot lawfully take a
   patient from you at all**, so every unregistered door you knock on is a guaranteed no.
   Filter to 강남/서초 의원급 dermatology. That is your entire universe.
2. **Hiring signals** — clinics posting on 사람인/잡코리아 for 중국어/일본어 가능 상담실장,
   국제진료 코디네이터 or 의료통역 are actively building intake capacity *this quarter*.
   That is the warmest cold lead available, and it is public.
3. **Behavioural signals** — clinics running Japanese/Chinese Instagram or RED (小红书)
   accounts, listed on 강남언니 global, or with a translated price page. They have
   already decided; they are shopping for a channel.
4. **Deprioritise** the top-tier 압구정 brand clinics. They have an in-house 국제진료팀 and
   existing agency contracts. Mid-tier clinics with capacity gaps and a fresh laser
   loan are where a new channel gets attention.

## 7. Fix #5 — If you do walk in, do it correctly

Only after §3. Rules:

- **Never ask for the 원장.** Ask for the **실장** or **마케팅 담당자** by title. Asking for
  the doctor marks you as someone who does not know how clinics work.
- **Timing:** weekday **2–4pm**. Not lunch, not 6–8pm (their peak), not Monday.
- **Do not request a meeting on the spot.** Ask for one thing only: the 담당자's
  카톡 ID or email so you can send the proposal. A small ask gets a yes.
- **Leave paper.** A single-page Korean 제안서 + 명함 with your 등록번호 printed on it.
  Paper survives after you leave the room; speech does not.
- **60 seconds, then leave.** Thank them and go. Follow up on KakaoTalk within 2 hours
  while the paper is still on the desk.

Scripts (walk-in, KakaoTalk, email, one-pager structure) are in `gtm/scripts_ko.md`.

## 8. Sequence

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Now → 4 weeks | Start 유치업자 registration (capital + 보증보험 + 사무소 + KORP filing). |
| Now → 10-30 | **Apply for a 협력업체 booth at 대한피부과의사회 추계학술대회** (Dec 11–13, COEX 마곡). Hard deadline. |
| Weeks 1–4 | Pull the KORP registry, build the 강남/서초 derm 의원 list; layer hiring + behavioural signals. Pick 30 targets. |
| Weeks 1–6 | Secure 5 real patients from the demand side. This is the actual bottleneck, not clinic access. |
| Weeks 4–8 | Work Door C: 3–5 coffee meetings with device reps / 개원 컨설턴트 in Gangnam. Ask for one introduction each. |
| Weeks 6–10 | Walk-ins resume — with registration number, one-pager, named 실장 targets, and 5 patients in hand. |
| Ongoing | KHIDI 유치사업자 컨설팅 지원사업; calendar Medical Korea (March) and SITMMT seller applications (August). |

The order matters. Registration turns a crime into a credential; demand turns a
favour into revenue; the conferences turn cold into warm. Walk-ins are the *last*
step, not the first.
