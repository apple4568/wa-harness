## 3. Category conventions (what everyone does)
- **Blue as default trust colour** across Korean tech (Toss, Channel Talk) and implied in medical SaaS; Jane is the lone teal outlier.
- **Rounded, friendly geometric sans** and soft symbols (speech bubble, palette, curved wordmark); "Bright & Light" / "친근한" are the recurring adjectives.
- **Count-based proof**: every brand leads with a number (3,527 병원 / 4,000+ practices / 16,000 physicians / 240k practitioners / 18만 기업 / 4,900 병원).
- **"All-in-one / 올인원 / 통합" as the category word** (KR: 연동; West: all-in-one practice management).
- **Compliance badges** as hygiene, rarely as story.
- **AI is the 2025–26 headline** for Nextech (Cora), Pabau (AI notes), Channel Talk (ALF), Dr.Palette (AI skin analysis partner).
- No direct competitor observed using a dark or editorial visual system.

## 4. White space for a bold, technology-forward, reliable Korean clinic OS (no AI-led messaging)
1. **"Operating system" framing is unclaimed.** Incumbents say 차트, CRM, 플랫폼; nobody in Korea says "운영체제/OS" for the clinic. Own the systems-level language: uptime, sync, one source of truth.
2. **Reliability as the hero.** Stability is the top selection factor (48%), security the top cloud fear (38.5%), outage the top barrier (48.4%) — yet no competitor puts uptime, offline fallback or data residency above the fold.
3. **A non-blue, non-pastel palette.** Blue is saturated (Toss, Channel Talk); teal is Jane; palette-multicolour is Dr.Palette. A dark, high-contrast graphite/ink system with one hot signal colour would be unique in this set.
4. **Precision typography** (tight, engineered sans with tabular numerals) versus the rounded-friendly norm — signals instrument, not toy.
5. **Real product, real density.** Show receptionist/consultant screens at working density rather than hero mockups.
6. **Aesthetic-clinic specificity** in Korean terms: 상담실장, 비급여, 시술 패키지, 알림톡 규정, 네이버예약.
7. **Compliance-forward Korean proof**: EMR 인증(2주기), ISMS-P, 개인정보 처리 기준, 알림톡 정보성/광고성 자동 분류 — turn regulation into a product feature.
8. **Foreign-patient readiness** (MOHW: 2M foreign patients in 2025) — multilingual reception/consent is a growth message no direct competitor leads with.

## 5. Buyer concerns — Korean clinic directors
**DOCUMENTED**
- EMR choice is driven by "제품·회사 안정성" (48.16% of 1,030 physicians); cloud hesitancy: data security 38.5%, cost 22.07%, unverified stability 19.25%, uncertainty 13.77%, few peers using it 6.42% — Medigate News survey.
- Cloud-EMR barriers among 839 hospital staff: internet outage/system stop 48.4%, medical-data security 37.6%, customisation limits 30.3%; 63.5% expect the shift to cloud — EZCaretech survey via 청년의사.
- 80% of clinics switching to cloud had experienced on-premise server problems — Medigate.
- Saturation: 85.3% of 2025 GP openings declared dermatology, 50 in Gangnam; 65% of new 피부과 had zero insured claims — 서울신문 / 의학신문.
- Non-covered price erosion (e.g., 점 빼기 2–3만원 → 1만원대) and "생존 경쟁" — 데일리메디, 청년의사.
- Messaging regulation: 알림톡 is 정보성 only; promo wording triggers (광고) labelling, night limits, 2-year re-consent; 의료광고 사전심의 — Omago / BlinkAd.
- Patient-data liability: a hospital leaking ~17,000 records led to fines for doctor and corporation — 팜뉴스.
- EMR 인증제 2주기 (2025.01–2027.12) adds interoperability standards — 보안뉴스 / MOHW.
- Foreign patients passed 2M in 2025 — MOHW press release.
- No-show: "비급여 병·의원 평균 노쇼·당일취소 10–30%" appears only in a vendor blog (세이프닥) — weak evidence.

**HYPOTHESIS (unverified)**
- Directors fear vendor lock-in and painful chart migration when leaving 스마트닥터-class incumbents.
- Counselor (상담실장) turnover makes CRM notes and package balances a continuity risk.
- Kakao/Naver/Instagram inquiry fragmentation causes missed leads; Channel Talk's growth suggests demand.
- Fear of downtime during peak (weekend/evening) hours outweighs interest in new features.

## 6. Design implications
1. Name the category in KO/EN: "클리닉 운영체제 / Clinic OS" — avoid 차트, CRM, 플랫폼 as primary nouns.
2. Lead visuals with reliability proof (uptime %, backup cadence, data residency, ISMS-P/EMR 인증) in the hero, not a footer badge.
3. Choose a dark graphite/ink base with one signal accent that is not Toss-blue, Channel-violet or Jane-teal; test amber, signal green or coral.
4. Typography: an engineered grotesk with tabular figures (KO: Pretendard Variable or a tuned custom cut) — tight tracking, heavy display weights, no rounded terminals.
5. Symbol: a structural/system mark (grid, node, switch, spine) rather than a bubble, palette or heart; must work at 16px favicon and on dark.
6. Show real screens at working density in flat crops; no floating 3D phones, no stock smiling doctors.
7. Build a "numbers system": counts (clinics, messages/day, uptime) rendered in the same tabular type — the proof layer becomes a brand element.
8. Encode compliance visually: iconography for 정보성/광고성 message status, consent state, audit log — regulation as UI.
9. Motion language: instant, mechanical, precise (100–200 ms) — the opposite of playful bounce.
10. Korean-first copy voice: declarative, short, numeric ("접수 12초. 노쇼 -31%. 다운타임 0."), avoiding AI buzzwords entirely.
11. Provide a bilingual (KO/EN, later JA/ZH/TH) lockup from day one to signal foreign-patient readiness.
12. Keep one immutable brand colour and a strict logo rule set (Toss-style discipline) to project institutional seriousness.
