# Research 03 — Typography, Readability, Accessibility, Credibility, Logo & Motion Foundations
**Project:** Synaptix / 시넵틱스 bilingual (KO-first, EN-secondary) B2B brand system for Korean aesthetic clinics
**Date accessed:** 2026-09-14 · Findings are documented unless marked "(hypothesis)".

## 1. Korean/English typography for software brands — candidates, Figma availability, licensing

- **Pretendard — SIL OFL, commercial use, embedding and modification permitted; only standalone sale of the font is prohibited.** The repo states "모든 상업적 행위 및 수정, 재배포가 가능" except selling the font by itself. Built on Inter + Source Han Sans + M PLUS 1p; 9 static weights plus **Pretendard Variable**; variants **Pretendard JP**, **Pretendard Std** (Latin/Greek/Cyrillic) and **Pretendard GOV** (Korean public services); CDN delivery via jsDelivr/cdnjs/UNPKG with static, dynamic-subset and variable-subset CSS. Latest release: 1.3.9. [orioncactus/pretendard](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard) · [Pretendard GOV README](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard/tree/main/packages/pretendard-gov). The English README claims legibility "without requiring further adjustments of scaling, letter-spacing, optical adjustments" [Pretendard EN README](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard/blob/main/packages/pretendard/docs/en/README.md).
- **Pretendard is NOT on Google Fonts, so it is not in Figma's default font list.** Figma ships Google Fonts (and Apple SF) by default; anything else must be installed locally and, in the browser, exposed via the Figma Font Helper (the desktop app reads system fonts). Korean designers document this workflow for Pretendard, and a community plugin "Pretendard'em All" batch-switches text layers. [Figma Help — Add a font to Figma](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039956894-Add-a-font-to-Figma) · [Figma Help — Missing font alert](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039956994-Missing-font-alert-in-Figma-Design) · [Pretendard'em All plugin](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1182140304869680211/pretendardem-all). Consequence: bundle Pretendard OTFs plus an install note in the kit, and name a Google-Fonts fallback for external collaborators.
- **Google-Fonts Korean faces (available in Figma without install):** *Noto Sans KR* — SIL OFL 1.1, © Google, part of the Noto CJK family; Figma lists Noto CJK fonts under short names (e.g., "Noto Sans KR"). [Google Fonts — Noto Sans KR](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+KR) · [Figma Help — Add text in Chinese, Japanese, and Korean](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040449673-Add-text-in-Chinese-Japanese-and-Korean). *IBM Plex Sans KR* — SIL OFL 1.1 (repo license OFL-1.1; `@ibm/plex-sans-kr` package), on Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts. [IBM/plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex) · [Google Fonts — IBM Plex Sans KR](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+KR). *Nanum Gothic* (Sandoll, OFL) is also on Google Fonts. [Google Fonts — Nanum Gothic](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nanum+Gothic).
- **Other Korean open faces (all SIL OFL, none on Google Fonts → local install for Figma):** *Spoqa Han Sans Neo* — OFL 1.1; repo explicitly allows "로고, BI, CI, 임베드 등의 상업적 사용" with no fee; 5 weights (Thin/Light/Regular/Medium/Bold) in OTF/TTF and web CSS. [spoqa/spoqa-han-sans](https://github.com/spoqa/spoqa-han-sans). *SUIT / SUIT Variable* — OFL 1.1, Hangul derived from Source Han Sans (Bongodik), variable + webfont builds. [sunn-us/SUIT](https://github.com/sunn-us/SUIT) · [Noonnu — SUIT](https://noonnu.cc/en/font_page/845). *Wanted Sans* — OFL; seven weights + variable; Hangul based on Source Han Sans; Latin drawn by Wanted Lab ("Geometric with a heart, Humanist with a soul"); has dedicated webfont docs. [wanteddev/wanted-sans](https://github.com/wanteddev/wanted-sans).
- **Latin companions and numerals:** *Inter* — OFL 1.1, variable 100–900, includes `tnum` tabular numbers, slashed zero, contextual alternates; on Google Fonts (the repo notes the Google Fonts build can lag the upstream release). [rsms/inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter). *Geist / Geist Mono* — OFL 1.1, by Vercel with Basement Studio; entered the Google Fonts catalogue on 2 Oct 2024. [vercel/geist-font](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) · [Google Fonts — Geist Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist+Mono). *JetBrains Mono* — OFL 1.1, "free of charge, for both commercial and non-commercial purposes", 100–800 variable; on Google Fonts. [JetBrains/JetBrainsMono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) · [Google Fonts — JetBrains Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono). *IBM Plex Mono* — OFL 1.1, same repo as Plex Sans KR, so a Plex-based stack gives KR + Mono under one license and one Google Fonts source. [IBM/plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex).

## 2. Korean readability — size, line-height, tracking, mixed-script wrapping, numerals

- **Line-height for Hangul UI text clusters around 1.5–1.6 (mobile ≈155%), higher than the 1.2–1.3 often used for Latin.** A Korean mobile-typography guide puts mobile body line-height at "155% 내외"; a design dictionary gives a general UI range of 1.2–1.5×, with Hangul at the top of it. [brunch — 모바일 UI 타이포그래피 가독성](https://brunch.co.kr/@chulhochoiucj0/34) · [디자인베이스 — 타이포그래피](https://designbase.co.kr/dictionary/typography/). Recommendation for Synaptix: body 14–16px at 1.5–1.6, dense tables 13–14px at 1.45 **(hypothesis synthesised from the above)**.
- **Slightly negative letter-spacing is the Korean product-design norm.** The same guide gives 16–17pt body text "−0.3" and says tracking tightens as size grows. A Korean design-system survey (oh-my-design.kr) reports −0.2px to −0.4px as typical Korean type tokens (Toss/Finda-style), while Pretendard claims to need little adjustment. [brunch — 모바일 UI 타이포그래피](https://brunch.co.kr/@chulhochoiucj0/34) · [oh-my-design — Toss Bank tokens](https://oh-my-design.kr/design-systems/tossbank) · [Pretendard EN README](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard/blob/main/packages/pretendard/docs/en/README.md). Practical rule: −1% to −2% (≈ −0.2 to −0.4px at 16px) on headings/body, 0 for small labels **(hypothesis)**.
- **Use `word-break: keep-all` for mixed Hangul/Latin text.** MDN: with `keep-all`, "word breaks should not be used for CJK text" while non-CJK behaves as `normal`, so lines wrap at 어절 boundaries. A Korean engineering blog adds `text-wrap: balance` for headings and `pretty` for body, always after `keep-all` (alone, `balance` can split 어절). [MDN — word-break](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/word-break) · [Dale Seo — 한국어 줄바꿈 다듬기](https://daleseo.com/css-text-wrap/).
- **Numerals for dashboards: tabular figures + right alignment.** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (OpenType `tnum`) gives digits equal widths so columns align and ticking values do not jump; support >96%. Apply it to every numeric element in a data view. [MDN — font-variant-numeric](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/font-variant-numeric) · [DEV — Tabular numbers in CSS](https://dev.to/alanwest/tabular-numbers-in-css-font-variant-numeric-vs-monospace-hacks-25cn). Precedent: Toss Product Sans (Sandoll/Leedotype, 2020–21) weights numerals and Latin to Hangul proportions and ships both proportional and tabular figures. [Sandoll — Hot financial platforms' perspective on fonts](https://en.sandoll.co.kr/Story/?bmode=view&idx=19492476).
- **Korean government precedent:** KRDS adopts Pretendard GOV as the single default face for Korean and Latin, validating a one-family KO/EN strategy. [KRDS — 타이포그래피](https://www.krds.go.kr/html/site/style/style_03.html) · [brunch — KRDS 살펴보기](https://brunch.co.kr/@duotoneofficial/58).

## 3. Accessibility — WCAG 2.2, APCA, KWCAG 2.2, colour-blind-safe status colours

- **WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 (AA): text ≥ 4.5:1; large text ≥ 3:1, where large = ≥18pt (≈24px) or ≥14pt bold (≈18.66px).** SC 1.4.6 (AAA) raises this to 7:1 / 4.5:1. [W3C — Understanding SC 1.4.3](https://w3c.github.io/wcag21/understanding/contrast-minimum.html) · [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).
- **SC 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA): UI components (input borders, focus rings, toggles) and meaningful graphics (icons, chart lines, pie slices) ≥ 3:1 against adjacent colours; disabled controls are exempt.** [Deque — 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast](https://dequeuniversity.com/resources/wcag2.1/1.4.11-non-text-contrast) · [Knowbility — Exploring 1.4.11](https://knowbility.org/blog/2018/wcag21-1411contrast).
- **APCA (Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm) is the candidate method in the WCAG 3 draft, not a current conformance standard.** It reports Lc values weighted by font size and weight: Lc 15 non-text minimum, Lc 30 floor for any text, Lc 45 large text, Lc 60 body minimum, Lc 75 preferred body, Lc 90 thin/small text. Use it as a quality check; WCAG 2.x ratios remain the contractual baseline. [APCA — In a Nutshell](https://git.apcacontrast.com/documentation/APCA_in_a_Nutshell.html) · [APCA — Easy Intro](https://git.apcacontrast.com/documentation/APCAeasyIntro.html) · [Designsystemet.no — WCAG 3 contrast](https://designsystemet.no/en/best-practices/accessibility/contrast).
- **Korea: KWCAG 2.2 (KS X OT0003:2022) is the national standard used for 웹 접근성 품질인증.** 4 principles, 14 guidelines, 33 check items; contrast ≥ 4.5:1, relaxable to 3:1 for zoomable text; a separate item requires colour-independent perception (색에 무관한 콘텐츠 인식). [KWCAG 2.2 unofficial mirror](https://a11ykr.github.io/kwcag22/) · [KS X OT0003 PDF (WebSoul)](https://www.websoul.co.kr/accessibility/PDF/%ED%95%9C%EA%B5%AD%ED%98%95%EC%9B%B9%EC%BD%98%ED%85%90%EC%B8%A0%EC%A0%91%EA%B7%BC%EC%84%B1%EC%A7%80%EC%B9%A82.2.pdf) · [pxd — KWCAG 2.2 요약](https://tech.pxd.co.kr/post/%ED%95%9C%EA%B5%AD%ED%98%95-%EC%9B%B9-%EC%BD%98%ED%85%90%EC%B8%A0-%EC%A0%91%EA%B7%BC%EC%84%B1-%EC%A7%80%EC%B9%A8-2-2-228).
- **Status colours must never be the only cue (WCAG 1.4.1 Level A).** Pair each status with an icon, label or shape; ~8% of men and ~0.5% of women have CVD, mostly red–green — the success/error axis. [Section508.gov — Making color usage accessible](https://www.section508.gov/create/making-color-usage-accessible/) · [DigitalA11Y — SC 1.4.1](https://www.digitala11y.com/understanding-sc-1-4-1-use-of-color/). For categorical palettes the Okabe–Ito 8-colour set (Wong, *Nature Methods* 2011) is the CVD-safe reference; Carbon's data-viz palettes are curated for neighbour contrast and 3:1 non-text contrast. [Wong — Points of view: Color blindness](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.1618) · [Carbon — Data-viz color palettes](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/color-palettes/).

## 4. Brand credibility and trust — B2B SaaS / healthtech, plus Korean medical-advertising constraints

- **Ehrenberg-Bass / Romaniuk: distinctive brand assets are judged on Fame (share of buyers who link the asset to the brand) and Uniqueness (how exclusively it points to you); consistency over time is what builds mental availability, and the default answer to changing an asset should be "no".** [Ehrenberg-Bass — The Four Commandments](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/the-four-commandments-future-proofing-a-brands-identity) · [Ehrenberg-Bass — Brands need distinctive assets](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-need-distinctive-assets) · [Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive Brand Assets* (OUP 2018)](https://www.amazon.com/Building-Distinctive-Brand-Assets-Romaniuk/dp/0190311509). Implication: pick few assets (wordmark, symbol, one colour, one numeric type treatment) and repeat them rigidly across product, sales and clinic-facing templates.
- **NN/g — four credibility factors (Nielsen 1999, reaffirmed 2016): design quality, up-front disclosure, comprehensive and current content, and connection to the rest of the web.** [NN/g — Trustworthiness in Web Design: 4 Credibility Factors](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/) · [NN/g — Trust or Bust (1999)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/communicating-trustworthiness/).
- **Stanford Web Credibility Guidelines (Fogg, 2002; 4,500 participants): 10 rules —** easy verification of accuracy; show a real organisation; highlight expertise; show honest people behind the site; easy contact; professional/appropriate design; easy to use and useful; frequently updated; restraint with promotional content; avoid errors of all types. [Stanford Persuasive Tech Lab — Guidelines](https://credibility.stanford.edu/guidelines) · [Wikipedia — Stanford Web Credibility Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Web_Credibility_Project). Rules 9–10 map directly onto the brief's "avoid hype" requirement.
- **Security/compliance evidence is a purchase gate in B2B software, but often skipped in practice.** G2's 2024 Buyer Behavior Report: 81% of buyers consider security incidents when purchasing, yet 48% closed deals without a completed security/privacy assessment. [G2 Research — Security compliance trends 2025](https://research.g2.com/insights/security-compliance-trends-2025). Public "trust centres" are the emergent signal (Vanta: customers deflect 87% of inbound security reviews with one). [Vanta — Trust Center](https://www.vanta.com/products/trust-center). A visible security/compliance page (개인정보보호법, 의료법 record-keeping) is therefore a credibility asset for a clinic OS **(hypothesis: no Korea-specific buyer survey found)**.
- **Korean Medical Service Act (의료법) — advertising limits that clinic-facing templates must respect.** Art. 56 prohibits, among others, patient testimonials (치료경험담) or content likely to mislead about treatment effects, ads that "객관적인 사실을 과장", and omission of important information such as serious side effects. Art. 57 requires pre-review (자율심의) for ads in periodicals, outdoor/transit media, and internet/SNS media with ≥100,000 average daily users, run by the self-review bodies of 대한의사협회, 대한치과의사협회 and 대한한의사협회. [의료법 제56조 (CaseNote)](https://casenote.kr/%EB%B2%95%EB%A0%B9/%EC%9D%98%EB%A3%8C%EB%B2%95/%EC%A0%9C56%EC%A1%B0) · [의료법 제57조 (CaseNote)](https://casenote.kr/%EB%B2%95%EB%A0%B9/%EC%9D%98%EB%A3%8C%EB%B2%95/%EC%A0%9C57%EC%A1%B0) · [대한의사협회 의료광고심의위원회 — 심의대상](https://www.admedical.org/guide/target.do) · [의협신문 — 의료광고 과징금](https://www.doctorsnews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=140631). Consequence: template copy fields must avoid superlatives, "No.1" and guaranteed-result language, and carry a 심의필 badge slot.

## 5. Logo-system conventions — clear space, minimum size, mono/reversed, misuse

- **Clear space is defined by an element of the mark itself.** Slack: clear space ≥ the octothorpe height (horizontal) or one lozenge (stacked); partner logos sit ≥ one octothorpe away on the optical baseline. [Slack Brand Guidelines PDF](https://a.slack-edge.com/80588/marketing/img/media-kit/Slack-Brand-Guidelines.pdf) · [Slack — Brand terms](https://slack.com/terms-of-service/slack-brand). Atlassian: the capital "A" of the wordmark defines minimum clearance. [Atlassian Design — Logos](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos). GOV.UK: clear-space unit is the dot in the wordmark. [GOV.UK brand — Logo elements](https://brand.design-system.service.gov.uk/logo-system/logo-elements). The generic x-height/cap-height unit convention is widely documented. [BrandyHQ — Logo usage guidelines](https://brandyhq.com/blog/logo-usage-guidelines/).
- **Minimum size: ~24px high for digital, ~10mm for print is a documented norm.** Atlassian: 24px height digital / 0.5in print. [Atlassian Design — Logos](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos). GOV.UK: wordmark ≥50px wide, crown ≥16px wide, small-crown variant for favicons, print ≥10mm high. [GOV.UK brand — Logo web](https://brand.design-system.service.gov.uk/logo-system/web) · [GOV.UK brand guidelines](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/govuk-brand-guidelines). Guideline round-ups cite 24px icon / ~80px wordmark as common digital floors. [BrandyHQ](https://brandyhq.com/blog/logo-usage-guidelines/).
- **Misuse lists are short and concrete.** Atlassian forbids reduced legibility, unapproved colour combinations, drop shadows, complex backgrounds and outlining. Toss requires fixed background colours and margins, allows symbol or signature forms only, bans recolouring, restyling or combining with other graphics, and forbids rounded corners on 600×600px mini-app marks. [Atlassian Design — Logos](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos) · [토스 브랜드 리소스 센터](https://brand.toss.im/) · [앱인토스 — 미니앱 브랜딩 가이드](https://developers-apps-in-toss.toss.im/design/miniapp-branding-guide.html). Kakao's CI page exists but numeric rules were not retrievable. [Kakao — CI](https://www.kakaocorp.com/kakao/introduce/ci?lang=en).
- **Synaptix rule set (derived):** clear space = cap-height of "S" on all sides; min 24px digital / 10mm print for the lockup, 16px for the symbol; full-colour, one-colour black, reversed white, KO/EN lockup pair; ≥8 misuse examples **(hypothesis: proposal)**.

## 6. Motion principles — Material 3, IBM Carbon, Apple HIG

- **Material Design 3 duration tokens:** short1–4 = 50/100/150/200ms, medium1–4 = 250/300/350/400ms, long1–4 = 450/500/550/600ms, extra-long1–4 = 700/800/900/1000ms. Easing: standard `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1)`, standard-decelerate `(0,0,0,1)`, standard-accelerate `(0.3,0,1,1)`, emphasized-decelerate `(0.05,0.7,0.1,1)`, emphasized-accelerate `(0.3,0,0.8,0.15)`; "emphasized" is recommended for most transitions, "standard" where speed matters. [material-foundation/material-tokens motion.json](https://github.com/material-foundation/material-tokens/blob/json/json/motion.json) · [M3 — Easing and duration](https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/easing-and-duration/tokens-specs).
- **IBM Carbon duration tokens (productive UI):** fast-01 70ms (button/toggle), fast-02 110ms (fade), moderate-01 150ms (default transition), moderate-02 240ms (expansion, toast), slow-01 400ms (large expansion, important notifications), slow-02 700ms (background dimming, hero). Easing: productive standard `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9)`, entrance `(0,0,0.38,0.9)`, exit `(0.2,0,1,0.9)`; expressive standard `(0.4,0.14,0.3,1)`, entrance `(0,0,0.3,1)`, exit `(0.4,0.14,1,1)`. Carbon separates *productive* (task-focused) from *expressive* (celebratory) motion. [carbon/packages/motion/src/dtcg/motion.json](https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon/blob/main/packages/motion/src/dtcg/motion.json) · [Carbon — Motion overview](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/motion/overview/).
- **Apple HIG:** add motion purposefully ("don't add motion for the sake of adding motion"); never make animation the only channel for important information; minimise or remove it when Reduce Motion is on; avoid large oscillating, spinning or scaling motion (≈0.2Hz oscillation is flagged). HIG publishes no numeric durations. [Apple HIG — Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) · [Apple — Reduced-motion evaluation criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/reduced-motion-evaluation-criteria).
- **Working range for Synaptix:** micro-interactions 70–150ms, component state/expansion 150–250ms, page/overlay transitions 250–400ms, celebratory/expressive up to 700ms; honour `prefers-reduced-motion` **(hypothesis: synthesis of the three systems above)**.
