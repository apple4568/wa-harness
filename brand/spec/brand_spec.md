## 1. Positioning
- Category (KO/EN): **클리닉 운영체제 / The clinic operating system**
- One-liner KO: 시넵틱스는 피부과·성형외과·미용 클리닉의 접수, 상담, 시술, 결제, 재방문 관리를 하나의 시스템으로 잇는 클리닉 운영체제입니다.
- One-liner EN: Synaptix is the operating system for Korean aesthetic clinics — reception, consultation, treatment, payment and follow-up, connected in one system.
- Vision (product): whole-clinic coverage. Describe current capability only as "접수·상담·시술기록·결제·재방문 관리" generically; no specific integrations.
- Name meaning: Synapse = the point where a signal is handed from one cell to the next; -ix = system. The brand is about **hand-offs between the parts of a clinic** (desk → consult → treatment → payment → follow-up).
- GUARDRAIL (from identity audit): name collides with Synaptics Inc. and several AI brands that use neural/brain/mesh imagery. Synaptix must NOT use neural-network meshes, brains, sparks, or many-to-many node webs. The symbol shows ONE clear path between TWO points; the link-grid motif stays sparse (max 2 connectors per node, never a mesh). Read as workflow/infrastructure, not AI.
- Prior state (audit): no public identity existed; internal positioning was a WhatsApp intake/qualification tool for ~26 Gangnam-area clinics serving English-speaking patients. The whole-clinic OS positioning widens the audience to 원장/실장 (director/manager) and the promise from a point tool to whole-clinic continuity; proof needs shift to breadth, reliability, data-handling clarity and pilot clinics.

## 2. Brand promise
KO: **클리닉의 모든 순간이 하나로 연결됩니다.**
EN: **Every moment of the clinic, connected as one.**

## 3. Value pillars (3)
1. **연결된 운영 · Connected operations** — 접수부터 재방문까지 한 기록, 한 흐름. One record, one flow, from front desk to follow-up.
2. **믿을 수 있는 정확성 · Dependable precision** — 숫자와 상태가 정확하고, 누락되지 않습니다. Numbers and statuses you can trust; nothing gets lost.
3. **명료한 속도 · Clear speed** — 클릭은 줄이고, 인수인계는 빠르게. Fewer clicks, faster hand-offs, less friction at the desk.

## 4. Tone of voice
명료하게 · 구체적으로 · 과장 없이 (Clear · Specific · Unhyped). Confident and direct; plain Korean (해요체 in product, 합니다체 in sales/legal). 
Do: name the workflow step, state what the software does, use real numbers only when real. 
Don't: "혁신", "최고", "AI가 알아서", superlatives, medical outcome claims (의료광고 규제 유의), exclamation marks.

## 7b. Documented buyer concerns to answer (from research/02_competitors.md — cite there)
- Stability is the #1 EMR selection criterion (48.16%, Medigate survey n=1,030). Cloud fears: security 38.5% / cost 22% / unproven 19%. Outage 48.4% / security 37.6% / customisation 30.3% (EZCaretech n=839).
- Korean 알림톡 정보성/광고성 rules, 의료법 Art. 56/57 medical-ad limits (no testimonials, no superlatives, 심의필 slot on ads).
- 85.3% of 2025 GP openings are 피부과 → crowded, price-eroding market; ~2M foreign patients → bilingual readiness.
- Hypotheses (label as such): vendor lock-in fear, 상담실장 turnover, channel fragmentation (Kakao/WhatsApp/LINE/phone), peak-hour downtime anxiety.
→ Messaging answers with specifics and process (안정성 설계, 데이터 처리 방침, 도입 절차, 지원 체계) — never invented uptime %, certifications or customer counts.

## 8. Creative routes (all three get: symbol sketch, wordmark, palette, type, 2 application tiles)
### Route A — 동기화된 운영 · Synchronized operations  ✅ SELECTED
- Symbol "The Link S" (연결 S): two circular nodes joined by one thick S-curve path; terminals ARE the nodes. Reads as "S" small, as "one hand-off, start to finish" large. Explicitly not a neural mesh.
- Wordmark: heavy geometric grotesk, tight tracking; the dot of "i" replaced by a node circle.
- Palette: Ink #0B1020 · Cobalt #2436E0 · Pulse Mint #31E3B5 · light neutrals.
- Type: Pretendard (KO/EN) + mono numerals.
- Motif: "Link grid" — modules joined by short connectors; coordinated motion (nodes snap together).
### Route B — 정밀 인프라 · Precision infrastructure
- Symbol: monoline "S" built from two parallel rails with square terminals inside a hairline square frame; crosshair ticks.
- Wordmark: technical grotesk, wide tracking, small caps "SYNAPTIX".
- Palette: Graphite #1E2229 · Electric Cobalt #1B37F2 · Safety Orange #FF6A1A · concrete greys.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans KR + IBM Plex Mono; visible grid lines, dimension annotations.
### Route C — 자신 있는 진보 · Confident progress
- Symbol: forward-leaning "S" slash arrow (italic stencil) with a vivid lime block.
- Wordmark: expressive extra-bold, slightly italic "Synaptix", soft rounded corners.
- Palette: Off-white #FAF8F2 · Ink #14161A · Vivid Lime #C6F135 · Coral #FF5A5F.
- Type: Wanted Sans / SUIT style rounded bold; big friendly headlines.

### Evaluation (1–5) — reliability · robustness · tech character · distinctiveness · KO readability · application usefulness · TOTAL
- A: 5 · 5 · 5 · 4 · 5 · 5 = **29**
- B: 5 · 4 · 4 · 3 · 4 · 3 = 23  (strong stability, but reads like industrial/dev-tool; annotations clutter clinic-facing pages; low ownability — many infra brands use rails/crosshairs)
- C: 3 · 3 · 4 · 4 · 4 · 4 = 22  (bold and modern, but drifts toward consumer apps (Toss / Gangnam Unni territory) — hypothesis: clinic directors may read it as less serious; lime fails text contrast)
Selection rationale (record in Figma): A embodies the name (synapse = connection), the product vision (whole clinic linked), and gives one ownable asset (Synapse S + link grid) that scales from favicon to dashboard nodes. It carries B's structure (grid, mono numerals) without the industrial coldness. Recorded as autonomous decision per brief.

## 11. Typography (FINAL — confirmed by Figma font probe, logs/00_scaffold.md)
Primary family (KO+EN): **IBM Plex Sans KR** — Figma family string "IBM Plex Sans KR", styles: "Thin","ExtraLight","Light","Regular","Text","Medium","SemiBold","Bold". SIL OFL 1.1, on Google Fonts (renders in Figma and any browser without install).
Numerals / data / labels: **IBM Plex Mono** — family "IBM Plex Mono", styles Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold (+ Italic). Tabular figures by default. OFL.
Wordmark base: **Space Grotesk** "Bold" (OFL, Google Fonts) — flattened to vector and customised; KO wordmark 시넵틱스 from IBM Plex Sans KR "Bold" (compare stroke weight against Space Grotesk Bold; if too light use Noto Sans KR "Black" and record which was used).
Why not Pretendard: not installed in this Figma environment (probe), and the category (Smart Doctor, Dr.Palette, Toss, Channel Talk) converges on Pretendard/Noto-class grotesks; Plex Sans KR gives an engineered, technical voice that is distinctive while remaining highly legible in Hangul. Pretendard (OFL) is an approved alternative for engineering teams that already ship it — metrics differ, so do not mix within one surface.
Scale (size/line-height, weight, tracking) — KO line-height ≥1.5 for body:
- Display 64/76 Bold −2% · H1 48/60 Bold −1.5% · H2 32/42 Bold −1% · H3 24/32 SemiBold −0.5% · H4 20/28 SemiBold 0
- Body L 18/28 Regular · Body 16/26 Regular · Body S 14/22 Regular · Caption 12/18 Medium +1%
- Numeric L 32/40 Plex Mono Medium · Numeric 16/24 Plex Mono Regular · Label 12/16 Plex Mono Medium +4% uppercase (EN only)
Rules: Hangul is primary — mixed lines use the same size, EN never smaller. Keep-all line breaking for KO. Max line length 45 KO chars / 75 EN chars. Numbers in dashboards use mono tabular.
Licensing note: IBM Plex Sans KR / IBM Plex Mono / Space Grotesk / Noto Sans KR — SIL OFL 1.1: free for commercial use, web embedding and app bundling; may not be sold on its own; reserved font names apply to modified versions. (Citations in research/03.)

## 12. Layout, spacing, motif, icon, image, motion
- Grid: Desktop 1440 → 12 col, 80 margin, 24 gutter, max content 1280. Tablet 1024 → 8 col. Mobile 390 → 4 col, 20 margin, 16 gutter.
- Spacing scale (4-base): 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96 128. Radius: 4 (inputs) 8 (cards) 12 (panels) 999 (pills/nodes).
- Graphic motif "Link grid": nodes (circles r 6–12) on a 48 px grid joined by 2 px connectors; density low; used as hero background, section dividers, chart node style. Secondary motif: "Signal path" — one thick S-curve stroke cropped large.
- Iconography: 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, round caps, node-shaped terminals on connection icons; outlined style; filled only for status.
- Imagery: real clinic operations (desk, consult, treatment prep), natural light, Ink overlay for text; no stock smiles, no before/after (medical ad regulation), no faces of patients.
- Motion: "Snap, don't float" — micro 150–200 ms, transitions 250–350 ms, ease-out cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1); motion shows linking (nodes connect, paths draw on); no bounce; honour reduced-motion.
